When Your System Fails in Sacramento

A few minutes of caution before we arrive can prevent a much bigger repair. An iced-over AC should be turned off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. Running it frozen risks an overflowing drain pan and a damaged compressor. A furnace that keeps short-cycling, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us immediately.

Keeping Your Sacramento HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
